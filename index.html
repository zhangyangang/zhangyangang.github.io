Yangang Zhang 张彦刚

AI & Robotics Researcher · Engineering Leader

I have nearly two decades of experience in artificial intelligence, computer vision, computing systems, and robotics. For the past decade, I have focused on robotics research, engineering, and team building—from algorithms and models to complete systems operating in the real world.

I received both my B.E. and M.E. in Electronic Information from Zhejiang University. My current interests include embodied foundation models, generalist robot learning, scalable robot data systems, and the translation of research into reliable products.
