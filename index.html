About

I am a robotics technology leader with nearly two decades of experience in artificial intelligence, computer vision, computing systems, and robotics. Over the past decade, I have focused on robotics R&D, team building, and full-stack system deployment.

I received my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Electronic Information from Zhejiang University. My interests include embodied foundation models, robot learning, multimodal reasoning, navigation and manipulation, and scalable robotics systems.
